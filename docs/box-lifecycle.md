# Box lifecycle

Generated by `node scripts/box-state-table.mjs > docs/box-lifecycle.md` from `src/router/box-state.mjs`. Every write of a box record's `state` goes through `transition(box, event, changes, now)`; an event not listed here does not exist, and an event applied to a box outside its source states writes nothing.

## States

23 states. Terminal: deleted. Lease boxes (not pool units, not in the cap or a lane): lease-booting, lease-baselining, lease-fencing, lease-resetting, leased, lease-returning, lease-warm.

## Supply

Supply is derived from the records on every tick and never stored as a count. A box is supply only in:

- booting, baseline: for 300 s after `since`
- ready: always
- registering: for 120 s; resetting: for 120 s
- idle: while the runner is online, or for 120 s after start before it was ever online
- busy, finished: only when a reset is pending (reusable; the runner was seen idle, or absent for less than 120 s, or its job was listed as completed; and the job result does not guard the box)

Each pool status (`pool:status:<label>`) carries the counts of the last tick for observers; the next tick recomputes them.
